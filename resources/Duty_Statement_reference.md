# Reference Duty Statement (existing, approved): Associate Governmental Program Analyst

Use this as the STRUCTURE reference when drafting a new duty statement. It is an approved, published duty statement for a related classification. Copilot should follow this structure and tone, not copy its content.

**Department:** California Department of Water Resources (DWR)
**Division:** Division of Flood Management
**Classification:** Associate Governmental Program Analyst (AGPA)
**Working Title:** Program Analyst, Flood Project Support
**Supervision Received:** Under the general direction of the Staff Services Manager I.
**Percent of time is shown for each essential function. Totals equal 100%.**

## Essential Functions

**40%, Program analysis and reporting.** Compiles, validates, and analyzes program and project data. Prepares monthly and quarterly status reports for division management and identifies schedule or budget risks.

**25%, Project tracking and coordination.** Maintains the division project tracker and milestone log. Coordinates with project teams to gather status and reconcile reporting differences.

**20%, Documentation and correspondence.** Drafts memos, briefing materials, and correspondence. Ensures documents meet state formatting and records-retention requirements.

**10%, Meetings and stakeholder support.** Prepares agendas and materials, records action items, and follows up on assignments.

**5%, Other duties as assigned.**

## Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities
- Knowledge of state program and project management practices.
- Ability to analyze data, reason logically, and present findings clearly in writing.
- Proficiency with Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Word, Excel, Teams).

## Work Environment
Full-time. Hybrid schedule with on-site work in Sacramento. Requires the ability to obtain and maintain a state background clearance.
