# Practice Inbox Pack — DWR Copilot Workshop

Four short, synthetic email threads for the Outlook exercises. Because Copilot in Outlook reasons over **mail you can open**, everyone needs real messages in their own mailbox. Before the session, email each thread to **yourself**:

1. Create a new message **to yourself**, set the **subject line exactly as written below**, paste **Message 1**, and send it.
2. **Reply** to that message (to yourself) with **Message 2**, then reply again with **Message 3**, so the thread builds up the way a real conversation would.
3. Repeat for all four subjects.

Use the **subject lines exactly** — you will point Copilot at each thread by typing `/` in the Copilot chat pane and selecting the subject.

> **Everyone and everything below is fictional.** These are made-up colleagues, vendors, and numbers for training only. Do not add real DWR data, real names, personnel information, or procurement-sensitive details. "You" are a DWR analyst on the North Delta Levee Rehabilitation project; the other people are your fictional teammates.

**The cast (all fictional):**

- **Priya Nair (Program Manager)** — your supervisor on the project
- **Daniel Cho (Finance)** — cost analysis and budget ceilings
- **Tanya Brooks (Contracts)** — task orders and approvals
- **Hector Salas (Environmental Compliance)** — permits and mitigation
- **Cordova Geotechnical** and **Redbank Engineering** — design consultants
